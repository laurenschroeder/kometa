Concept

Making an accessible VR game that people describe as magical

This VR experience invites players into a minimal, stylized cosmos where they guide a comet on its journey. I wanted to make something that's accessible for anyone to play, even if they've never tried VR, while making the experience feel magical.

{{CORNERS}}

Intention

One gesture, felt in the hand

Kometa uses a custom spring-joint system to give players intuitive, physical control over an orbiting ball that represents their comet. The spring joint creates a satisfying sense of weight and momentum: swinging wide builds speed, pulling close creates tight spirals.

This single mechanic is the foundation for every interaction in the game, recontextualized across all five chapters.

{{CORNERS}}

Universe

The lifecycle of a comet

I was inspired in learning about the lifecycle of comets, and how they have seeded planets, possibly Earth, with some of the elements needed to create life.

In the experience you begin as stardust, gather yourself into a comet with the motion of your hand, and carry what you choose (souls, living things, or volatile gasses) to a young planet. What you seed there grows into an ecosystem, shapes the fate of its people, and finally decides where your comet goes.

Phase 1

Stardust

You begin as scattered stardust. Sweeping your hand through the dark draws the motes together into a glittering clump that trails behind you.

{{CORNERS}}

Phase 2

Pebbles

Three kinds of matter appear around you: soul dust drifts as translucent, wobbling islands; organic matter as speckled stones; volatile gasses as glowing clouds. What you gather forms your comet's body, and what you gather most gives it its face.

{{CORNERS}}

Phase 3

Seeding

You swing your comet around a young planet, raining stardust onto its surface. Years later, the world you seeded comes alive.

{{CORNERS}}

Phase 4

Fate

Up close, the planet's people live out the consequences of what you brought. Each kind of comet tells a different story. Shown here is a world seeded with volatile gasses, where a crowned King's court gathers in anger and grief.

{{CORNERS}}

Phase 5

Launch

Finally, you choose: stay in orbit around the world you made, or fling yourself into the great unknown. Then you let your comet go.
